Did the reply answer the QC question?

A new message and a useful answer are different events. Use this worksheet after requesting clarification about a received item. The result describes your private review status; it does not send a request, place a warehouse hold or approve a shipment.

Review the evidence you actually retained

No review yet. An unreadable label remains an unresolved label question.

Start with one observation

Write what is visible without supplying a missing explanation. “The photograph does not show the trousers' size label” is an observation. “The trousers are the wrong size” requires additional evidence. A clear view of the hoodie label does not resolve the trousers' label question, even if both pieces were offered together.

Turn the observation into a request that can be answered: a readable view of the selected pair's second label, tied to the received item. Before requesting extra photography or measurement, confirm the current availability and any charge. Keep full order references in the actual private service channel.

Evaluate the response against the original question

A wider photograph may improve context while leaving a small label unreadable. A close-up may make the label legible but omit the relationship to the whole item. Keep both context and detail when that relationship matters. Do not close a question merely because another photograph arrived.

If the evidence directly answers the question, record the answer and its limitations. An answered size-label question does not certify authenticity, long-term durability or comfort. It also does not approve other items in the parcel.

Keep review decisions separate from operational actions

You may decide to ask, compare or pause your own review. A note saying “pause” does not establish that a warehouse accepted a hold instruction. If time matters, confirm the operational action and current deadlines through the service that handles the order. This independent page cannot change an account or shipment.

Likewise, an order payment, warehouse receipt and carrier acceptance scan describe different events. A reply saying an item arrived should not silently become a record that an international carrier has accepted the parcel.
